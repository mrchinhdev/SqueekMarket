# 🐹 Squeek Market

### Bet on Squeektoshi · Hamster-Powered Predictions

[![Live Demo](https://img.shields.io/badge/Live%20Demo-Visit%20Website-blue?style=for-the-badge)](https://www.squeekmarket.xyz/)

**Squeek Market** is a hamster-powered prediction market built for the Squeekchain ecosystem.

It brings prediction markets to life through a simple and interactive experience where users can explore daily markets, make predictions, track their bets, compete on leaderboards, and earn badges.

Powered by Squeekchain's on-chain activity, Squeek Market combines blockchain technology, gamification, and community participation into one fun experience.

🌐 **Live Website:** https://www.squeekmarket.xyz/

---

## ✨ Features

### 📊 Daily Prediction Markets
- Explore daily prediction markets.
- Participate in available prediction challenges.
- Use free daily points to place predictions.
- Track market activity and outcomes.

### 💰 Betting System
- Receive 12 free points every day.
- Each prediction costs 1 point.
- Use $SQK for unlimited bets.
- Connect your wallet to access the platform.

### 🏆 Leaderboard
- Track your prediction activity.
- Compete with other community members.
- Discover top-performing participants.

### 🎖️ Badges & Rewards
- Explore achievement badges.
- Participate in the platform's gamified experience.
- Build your prediction history and achievements.

### 🔗 Squeekchain Integration
- Connect wallets through Wagmi.
- Built for Ethereum Sepolia testnet.
- Display live blockchain statistics.
- Retrieve settled work from the official Squeekchain API.
- Automatically check and resolve eligible markets.

### 📈 Live Network Statistics
Monitor Squeekchain activity directly from the dashboard, including:
- Current block height.
- Settled work.
- Wheel activity.
- Market resolution status.

## 🏗️ Architecture

Squeek Market uses a Next.js application with API routes handling market and betting data.

- **Frontend:** Interactive prediction market dashboard.
- **Wallet Layer:** Wagmi and Viem for Ethereum wallet connectivity.
- **Backend:** Next.js API routes.
- **Storage:** JSON-based file storage for markets and bets.
- **Blockchain Data:** Official Squeekchain API.

**Official API:** https://api.squeekchain.com
